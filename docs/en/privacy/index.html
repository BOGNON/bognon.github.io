Privacy policy

Updated on 28 October 2026.

In short

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. It has no database: the forms store nothing.

Data controller

Isaac BOGNON, Porto-Novo, Benin, isaacbognon@icloud.com.

Data you send me

The contact and quote forms open your email app or WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. If you send it, I receive the information you chose to include (name, email, phone number, project description). I use it only to reply, prepare a quote and follow up on our business relationship. I never sell or share it.

These exchanges go through the services you use (your email provider, Apple iCloud for my inbox, WhatsApp for messages), which apply their own privacy policies.

Retention

I keep your messages for as long as needed to handle your request and follow up on any work, and delete them on request.

Hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any host, GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Offline use

To let you read the site offline, your browser keeps a copy of its pages and images in local storage. These files stay on your device and contain no personal data. You can clear them from your browser settings.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data by writing to me at isaacbognon@icloud.com. You can also contact Benin’s data protection authority (APDP).
