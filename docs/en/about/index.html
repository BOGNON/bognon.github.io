About

Isaac BOGNON, web developer and entrepreneur

A business owner, founder of LeCodeTech and web developer with a passion for technology, design and entrepreneurship, I put my creativity and skills to work for innovative projects.

I design and build business websites, online shops and web platforms, from interface design to deployment. Based in Porto-Novo, Benin, I welcome projects from clients and partners worldwide.

I am also the author of the book “La Mine d'Or des Entrepreneurs” (The Entrepreneurs' Gold Mine), a collection of business ideas suited to the African market, written to guide and motivate young entrepreneurs.
