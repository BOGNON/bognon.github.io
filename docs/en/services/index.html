04FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

Every project is priced on a quote: the price depends on the number of pages, the features (catalogue, ordering, customer area…) and the content to produce. The quote is free and with no commitment.

How long will my project take?

The timeline depends on the size of the project and on how quickly the content (text, photos, logo) is provided. It is stated clearly in the quote.

Do you work with clients outside Benin?

Yes. Based in Porto-Novo, I welcome projects from clients and partners worldwide. We communicate by email and on WhatsApp.

Will my site work on phones?

Yes. Pages are designed for mobile first, then adapted to tablets and computers.

Will I be able to update my site myself?

Depending on your needs, the site can include a content management tool, with a walkthrough at delivery. Otherwise, I can handle updates as part of maintenance.

Who handles hosting and the domain name?

I advise you on hosting and the domain name, then set up and publish the site. Hosting and domain fees are paid to the provider and are separate from the build.

How does payment work?

This site does not take any online payment. Payment terms are agreed directly with you and stated in the quote.

How do I order the book “La Mine d’Or des Entrepreneurs”?

From the catalogue, choose “Order on WhatsApp” or “Order by email”: a pre-filled message opens, and I reply with the price and details.
